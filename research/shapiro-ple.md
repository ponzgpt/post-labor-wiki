# David Shapiro's Post-Labor Economics: framework map

Working notes for representing Shapiro's ideas faithfully. Paraphrased; every term links to where he defines it. Checked 2026-10-25.

## Primary material (cite these, in this order)
1. *Labor Zero: AI, Automation, and the Last Employed Generation* (2026), the book. Online companion with appendix, 82-page bibliography and the four front plates [@shapiro2026laborzero]. Companion is © all rights reserved: **link, never copy**.
2. "Understanding Post-Labor Economics in Six Easy Steps" (Substack, July 2025), his own intro [@shapiro2025six].
3. Labor/Zero movement posts: "I'm starting a movement" [@shapiro2025movement], "Technofeudalism is the Default Trajectory" [@shapiro2026technofeudalism], "Why Ending Labor is Catastrophic for Democracy" [@shapiro2026democracy].
4. Public research repo, CC0 [@shapiro-ple-repo]; Universal High Income whitepaper and simulator [@shapiro-uhi-repo].

His repo README says the `/Research` PDFs are AI deep-research outputs and that he wants no PRs or feedback there. So: mine them for leads, then cite the primary source they point to. Never cite them for a fact.

## Core concepts (paraphrased)
| Term | What it means in PLE | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Post-Labor Economics | Study and design of an economy where GDP growth no longer runs through wage employment, and of the institutions that turn the automation surplus into broad, property-based income | Six Steps; repo chart "The Great Decoupling" |
| The Great Decoupling | The irreversible split between economic output and human labour (book's working title) | Book plates 1–2; Substack "Labor/Zero Updates" |
| Wage-based → capital-based system | Today households earn by working; after the decoupling they earn mainly by owning | Book plates 1–2 |
| Labor/Zero (L/0) | The movement: end *compulsory* labour; work becomes optional, ownership replaces wages as the main way income is distributed | "I'm starting a movement" |
| Laborism | His word for the cross-partisan belief that human labour must be kept as economic necessity, virtue or identity; he traces it to the Protestant work ethic [@weber1905] | "I'm starting a movement" |
| Economic agency | A household's ability to secure its living; the Economic Agency Index splits household income into wages, property returns and transfers | Six Steps, part IV |
| Pyramid of Prosperity | Five tiers of post-labor household income, bottom to top: universal basic income → public wealth-fund dividends → collectively owned private assets → personally accumulated assets → residual wages | Book plate 3; repo chart "PLE pyramid of income" |
| Pyramid of Power | Five layers of a new social contract. **Layer names not yet verified: take them from plate 4 before writing that page** | Book plate 4 |
| Levers of power | Labour rights are fading as a lever; property rights and democratic rights (plus proposed "algorithmic rights") must carry the load | Repo charts "Levers of Power", "Algorithmic Rights" |
| Technofeudalism as default | Without new participation institutions the default outcome is concentration, not utopia | Substack, Jan 2026 |
| "Necessary but not sufficient" | UBI alone does not fix the power problem; prosperity measures need power measures | Six Steps, part V |
| Solarpunk vs cyberpunk | The post-labor future is path-dependent; policy chooses the attractor | Six Steps, part VI |

## Mechanisms he names
Sovereign/public wealth funds and automation dividends [@nbim2024] [@apfc]; expanded ESOPs (lineage: [@kelso1958]); universal basic capital grants (lineage: [@paine1797]); public equity stakes in AI built on public data; universal basic services [@ucl2017]; DAOs, quadratic voting and blockchain governance as power tools. Precedents he cites: Norway, Alaska, Singapore.

## How to earn his approval (our rules)
- Separate **empirical** claims (labour share, LFPR, trials) from **normative** ones (what we should build) and from **forecasts** (robot counts, job-loss curves). Label forecasts and his "illustrative" charts as such.
- Every number traces to primary data or a peer-reviewed paper, with a locator.
- Steelman the skeptics ([@autor2015] [@mokyr2015] [@benanav2020] [@acemoglu2024]); Shapiro's own repo has "Arguments Against the Job Automation Apocalypse", so he engages with them too.
- Don't frame PLE as communism or as "just UBI". He positions it as market-compatible and explicitly beyond UBI.
- Credit his CC0 assets by name and link; never present his frameworks as ours.
