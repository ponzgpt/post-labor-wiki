# Visual assets: sources and licences

Rule: only public domain, CC0 or CC BY material, plus our own originals. Every image gets a credits entry (source URL, author, licence). No meme templates cut from films or stock photos.

## 1. Shapiro's PostLaborEconomics repo (CC0 1.0)
https://github.com/daveshap/PostLaborEconomics · charts index: https://daveshap.github.io/PostLaborEconomics/charts/ · memes: https://daveshap.github.io/PostLaborEconomics/memes/
- **Memes (27)**: "horse driving tractor", "farrier", "cubicle farm museum", "five computers world market", "biplane will never improve", "ai will be different this time", "gru meme economic agency paradox", and others. Use as is, credited.
- **Charts (41)**: reuse the *conceptual* ones (Pyramid of income, Levers of Power, Property-Based Income, The Great Decoupling card). Data charts that are "illustrative" or "stylized" (job displacement, robot ramps, deflation, office space) are forecasts: show them only labelled as Shapiro's scenarios, never as data.
- **Not CC0**: `docs/laborzero/` (book plates, appendix, bibliography) and video thumbnails. Link only.

## 2. Live data (redraw ourselves or embed)
- **FRED** (St. Louis Fed; BLS series are US public domain): PRS85006173 labour share, LNS11300060 prime-age LFPR, MANEMP manufacturing jobs, OPHNFB productivity. Embed the FRED graph or pull CSV at build time.
- **Our World in Data** (CC BY 4.0, iframe embeds allowed): share of labour in agriculture, annual working hours, robot installations.
- **EPI**: productivity–pay gap data (cite; check terms before redistributing the dataset).
