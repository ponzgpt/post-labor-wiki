<div class="q-grid">
  <article class="q"><h3>Is there a limit to what machines can do?</h3><p><strong>Yes, for now:</strong> task-based models find modest effects <Cite id="acemoglu2024" at="abstract" />. <strong>No:</strong> if the tasks humans can do are bounded, full automation makes wages collapse <Cite id="korinek2024" at="abstract" />.</p><p class="q-settle">Settled by: whether new tasks keep appearing faster than old ones are automated.</p></article>
  <article class="q"><h3>Is labour's share falling because of technology?</h3><p><strong>Yes:</strong> cheaper investment goods explain about half the global fall <Cite id="karabarbounis2014" at="abstract" />. <strong>No:</strong> harmonised data show stable shares outside the US and Canada <Cite id="gutierrez2020" at="abstract" />, and US concentration in superstar firms explains much of the rest <Cite id="autor2020superstar" at="abstract" />.</p><p class="q-settle">Settled by: consistent cross-country measures, tracked as AI spreads.</p></article>
  <article class="q"><h3>Are the first AI job effects real?</h3><p><strong>Yes:</strong> young workers in exposed jobs are falling behind <Cite id="brynjolfsson2025canaries" at="abstract" />. <strong>Not yet:</strong> earnings and hours show precise zeros in Denmark <Cite id="humlum2025" at="abstract" />.</p><p class="q-settle">Settled by: whether the gap spreads beyond entry-level hiring.</p></article>
  <article class="q"><h3>Cash, services, jobs or ownership?</h3><p><strong>Cash:</strong> it rarely stops people working <Cite id="banerjee2017lazy" at="abstract" />. <strong>Against:</strong> a meaningful UBI is very expensive <Cite id="hoynes2019" at="abstract" />. <strong>Ownership:</strong> PLE's preferred route, with the thinnest evidence (see the [toolbox](/policy/)).</p><p class="q-settle">Settled by: trials of ownership-based income at scale, which do not yet exist.</p></article>
  <article class="q"><h3>Does democracy need labour?</h3><p><strong>Yes:</strong> labour's leverage forced concessions, and income without leverage is dependency <Cite id="shapiro2026democracy" at="'The Central Reframe'" />. <strong>Not only:</strong> who benefits from technology has always been a political choice <Cite id="acemoglu2023" at="prologue" />.</p><p class="q-settle">Settled by: experience in places where most adults' income does not come from work.</p></article>
  <article class="q"><h3>What gives life meaning without a job?</h3><p><strong>Work matters:</strong> a guaranteed job raised status, time structure and purpose <Cite id="kasy2026" at="abstract" />. <strong>Other routes:</strong> people will still pay for human attention and presence, a meaning economy <Cite id="shapiro2026seven" at="intuition 7" />.</p><p class="q-settle">Settled by: wellbeing data from people with secure income and no obligation to work.</p></article>

<Tag kind="forecast" />Each question has a forecast on both sides; none is settled by the evidence so far. That is why this wiki labels claims, and why the [measures dashboard](/measures/) exists: the answers will show up in data before they show up in consensus.

## How to disagree well

- Name the measure: exposure, adoption, employment, hours, pay or income share are different things.
- Name the time frame: the past, the next five years and the long run need different evidence.
- Separate what *will* happen from what *should*: the first is a forecast, the second a proposal.
