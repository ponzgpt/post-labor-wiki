  <p><strong>Post-labor economics</strong> is the framing developed by <strong>David Shapiro</strong>: as AI and robots out-compete human labour, household income and citizens' leverage must come from ownership rather than wages. This wiki organises the field around his framework, describes his ideas in his terms and links to his work: <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@DaveShap">YouTube</a>, <a href="https://daveshap.substack.com/">Substack</a>, <a href="https://daveshap.github.io/PostLaborEconomics/">research</a> and <a href="https://daveshap.github.io/PostLaborEconomics/laborzero/"><cite>Labor Zero</cite></a>.</p>
