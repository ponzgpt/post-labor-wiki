<Tag kind="proposal" />Every instrument here is a proposal about what *should* be done; the evidence lines say how much is actually known. "Strong" means several randomised or quasi-experimental studies of the instrument itself; "thin" means proposals, costings or evidence from a narrower cousin.

Post-Labor Economics treats the first three as a floor and ownership as the main event <Cite id="shapiro2026seven" at="intuition 5" />. Others start from services, or from jobs. Each page ends with the strongest case against.
