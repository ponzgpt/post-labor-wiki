Post-labor arguments rest on a claim about the present: that human labour is already losing ground. These pages test that claim against official data. Charts are drawn from committed snapshots of FRED, the ILO, the OECD and Our World in Data, every chart has a data table and a CSV download, and each page ends with the strongest counter-reading.

<Aside>
The first four figures point toward a decoupling of output from labour. The last points the other way: so far, people are still working. Both are true, and the pages below show why.
</Aside>
